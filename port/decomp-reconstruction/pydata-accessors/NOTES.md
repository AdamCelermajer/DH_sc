## Structs records (`gen_structs.py`, wave 4)

- Generator: `python3 port/decomp-reconstruction/pydata-accessors/gen_structs.py --through N`. It
  reads the pseudocode of every queued `Structs::X` destructor (D2, D1, D0), finalize and read through
  `tools/decomp_extract.py --address`, so ledgered functions are included and the output is cumulative.
  Classes are taken in name order with their bases, until the function count reaches N. It writes
  `include/pydata-accessors/structs_records.hpp` and `src/structs_records.cpp` whole, and replaces only
  its own rows (source_file `pydata-accessors/src/structs_records.cpp`) in ledger.csv. `--dry-run` shows
  what was selected and why the other classes were left out.
- Accepted shapes (anything else leaves the class out, and its rows stay queued):
  - D2/D1: optional vtable store, optional `if (field != 0) CustomFree` blocks, base destructor last.
  - D0: own D1, then CustomFree. This is the deleting variant.
  - finalize: optional base finalize call and conditional CustomFree blocks, in listing order.
  - read: optional base read call and `StreamReader::readAs<T>` stores at fixed offsets, in order.
- A class is emitted only if its base is emitted (or is `ScriptCmd`), every base call names an ancestor
  that has the method in its emitted chain, a stored offset is not also freed, and no derived class
  redefines an ancestor offset. Qualified base calls (e.g. `Anim::read` calling `SimpleAnim::read`)
  stay qualified.
- Verification: all 529 functions were checked against the pseudocode for stores (offset and type),
  frees and the D0 shape, with zero mismatches. About 16 functions were hand-checked against the listing,
  covering D2, D1, D0, finalize with and without frees, reads with and without base calls, and
  int16/int32 stores. `structs_records_test` decodes SimpleAnim and Anim reads, checks a short stream,
  runs AnimStep finalize and the deleting destructor.
- Decisions:
  - D2 and D1 share one C++ destructor; both addresses are in its comment and both have ledger rows.
    The vtable store is compiler-generated, and the base destructor runs after the body as in the
    original. The original's `return param_1` is dropped (ABI detail).
  - D0 is the free function `Structs::DeletingDestructor(X*)` (overloaded per class). It calls
    `self->X::~X()` (non-virtual, as in the original) and then `CustomFree(self)`.
  - read and finalize are non-virtual methods. The original's vtable slots +0x8 (finalize) and
    +0xc (read) are not modelled; the Arrays port needs them virtual.
  - Freed words are `void*` (the CustomFree argument). finalize and the destructor do not reset them,
    as in the original.
  - Field names are `m_wN` (word N, offset 4*N) or `m_offN` for unaligned offsets. Comments carry the
    original 32-bit offset. Base fields are not repeated in derived classes.
  - Stored types come from readAs: int32 (`i`), uint32 (`j`), int16 (`s`), float (`f`). `bool` (`b`) is
    uncertain, so a read that stores one is `partial`: CharEffect, ItemPowerEntry, Projectile,
    RepeatableSprite, TriggerPlate.
- Layouts inferred (offsets are the original 32-bit ones):
  - `AIFaction`: int32 at +0x4 and +0x8, root, no-op destructor.
  - `SimpleAnim`: int16 at +0x4 and +0x6. `Anim : SimpleAnim`: int16 at +0x8 and +0xa, read calls SimpleAnim::read.
  - `AnimStep`: freed pointer at +0x24. `AIProps`: freed pointer at +0x2c.
  - `FriendlyNPCProperties : CharacterProperties`: 224 int32 words after the base read (no base read call).

## Remaining (1286 queued)

- Structs destructors (about 696 queued): the about 80 ScriptCmd command classes in `script_cmd.hpp` and
  ScriptCmd itself (skipped by the generator). Their freed words are uint32 members in the header, so they
  need retyping as `void*` first. Also the 113 classes with other control flow (below).
- Structs finalize (about 232) and read (about 225) with control flow the generator does not parse:
  - conditional reads `if (*(int *)(p + N) == 0) {...}` and `V = *(int *)(p + N)` loads;
  - element-array loops that call element vtables (e.g. `LangSheetList`, `Sound`, `SpawnGroup`);
  - reads whose base read is not in this category's queue: `v2Condition::read` (112 bytes) and
    `v2QuestReward::read`. They block 27 reads (`v2IsEventInState`, `v2IsQuestInState`, `v2QuestReward*` and similar).

  stride 0xc (finalize) or 0x18 (read) in the original. Needs the vtable-address-to-class mapping
  (`vtables-001.json`) and virtual finalize/read in the Structs port. Not started.
- PyDataArrays and PyDataConstants (about 11): destructors, Load, registerClassByName, the constructors,
  getConstantName and reloadData. Not started.
