## Logic evidence

The recovered v1.0.2 source is `port/level-world/reference/character-skill-combat-v6/original-functions.asm`. `Character::F_ApplyResult` at `0x3b10b4` first branches on the online mode at `0x3b10e4`; its local result path branches by `AttackResult` bits: Dodge `0x2` at `0x3b1258`, Block `0x4` at `0x3b1264`, Injure `0x10` at `0x3b126c`, Push `0x80` at `0x3b1274`, Stun `0x40` at `0x3b127c`, Fear `0x20` at `0x3b1304`, and Slow `0x100` at `0x3b136c`. Only the explicit Injure bit reaches `CharStateMachine::SM_SetInjureState` at `0x3b1924`; the second overload also calls it at `0x3b0358`. The existing recovered player-state handoff, projected in `port/level-world/player_injure_state_v7.cpp:10`, records that the setter at `0x3c5d84` returns when the shared Character gate `+0x14fc` is positive and writes 3000 ms before animation-table/clip lookup on a fresh request. The video does not expose that gate's value.

Caller admission was checked for the skill-combat path. `Character::_SkillCombatRoll` at `0x3b9fbc` validates the requested skill index against `GetCharSkillList`, resolves a non-null target handle/Character and the selected skill, checks main/offhand weapon availability for the weapon-required skill flag, then calls `F_SkillAttack` at `0x3b31a4` and `F_ApplyResult`. `F_SkillAttack` rejects missing attacker/target, chooses the equipped weapon from its offhand flag, adds the source skill formula flag, and calls `_F_CalculateResult` at `0x3b2638` before application. These are skill-caller gates; they do not establish the normal enemy-animation marker's production route. That caller still needs a main executable trace.

The current reconstructed policy carries the full source outcomes and mask through `CombatSession::apply_source_result` (`port/windows-foundation/combat_session.cpp`, around line 1527) to `ActorCombatRuntime::handle_applied_hit` and `start_injure_reaction` (`port/windows-foundation/actor_combat_runtime.cpp`, around lines 231–345). It starts Injury only when outcome bit `0x10` is present and honors the 3000 ms gate. Incoming-result handling does not yet dispatch source Push (`0x80`), Stun (`0x40`), Fear (`0x20`), or Slow (`0x100`) to reusable runtime effects; a Push-bearing result can therefore leave the attack active even though the recovered local path applies Push. The Session caller's source-result admission, generation and duplicate-delivery checks are in `combat_session.cpp` around lines 1498–1535. Lethal damage uses the existing death path. The separate calculated-hit kernel does not prove those Session policies by itself.

The corresponding recovered skill result provider is `port/level-world/character_skill_application_v116.cpp:105–118`: Push outcome `0x80` calls `skill_apply_push` with mask-derived direction/flags; Stun `0x40` uses attacker property 140 or 185 depending on mask `0x1000`; Fear/Scare `0x20` uses property 143 or 187 depending on `0x4000`; Slow `0x100` uses property 146 or 189 depending on `0x10000`. Injury is separately mask-gated by `0x18000000`. A minimal integration should keep these plain effects/timers in the incoming source-result owner, carry outcome+mask through the existing admission/generation/duplicate gates, and obtain source-backed property durations at the admitted result caller. It need not recreate the recovered Character object graph. These provider/property branches are recovered for skill application; the normal enemy animation-marker caller has not yet been traced, so the test below establishes a current Session omission under a controlled result, not that the forced result occurs in the ordinary shipped encounter.

## Expected behavior

Apply admitted source damage once and retain all outcome bits. Miss, Dodge, Block, and clean ordinary nonlethal damage must not reset the player's attack pose, attack target, or attack clock unless the result independently contains a source reaction bit. Injury follows its shared gate; Push/Stun/Fear/Slow follow their own recovered source effects and duration providers. Lethal damage must enter the existing Died pose. The reusable implementation is one source-result admission path that forwards damage, outcomes, mask, source generation, and event index into the existing Session/runtime owner.

## Uncertainties

The original video is v1.0.3 while the recovered source and assets are v1.0.2; a behavioral difference is possible and has not been measured. The video's red burst cannot be assigned to the Warrior, and the HP bar change does not reveal the source result category. The focused source search produced no Dodge or Block outcome for the tested `Swamp_LizadMan_Type1` against the selected Knight/Rogue loadouts across seeds 1–49,999, including a Knight with source `Shield01`; the test reports those branches as unavailable rather than manufacturing result flags. This is a bound on that test fixture only. The normal executable's production incoming-hit caller, casts, and the reported user's exact failure have not been traced here.
