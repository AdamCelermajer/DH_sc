These are sampled stills: they establish the controls and menu context, but do not show a click transition, sound timing, or acoustics. The v1.0.3 visual reference and v1.0.2 recovered SWF may differ.

**Logic evidence.** The decoded v1.0.2 SWF source is `port/engine-ui/reference/character-menu-flow-v1/authored-actions.txt`; source cue bindings are in `port/engine-audio/reference/source-bindings-v38/ledger.json`.

- Details `btn_EquipItem.onRelease` starts at `0x1D907`. It calls `NativeInvEquipItem(ItemID, InvSlotId)` at `0x1D936`, then regenerates the list and calls `DisplayEquipedItem` (`0x1D942`–`0x1D96E`). It then unconditionally maps `InvSlotId` 1 or 2 to `PickupWeapon` (`0x1D99E`), 9 to `PickupPotion` (`0x1D9C8`), and other slots to `PickupArmor` (`0x1D9DB`). There is no native-result branch between the call and cue.
- Details `btn_Unequip.onRelease` starts at `0x1D52A`. It maps `InvSlotId` 1/2 to `PickupWeapon` (`0x1D569`), 9 to `PickupPotion` (`0x1D593`), and other slots to `PickupArmor` (`0x1D5A6`), then calls `NativeInvUnequipItem(InvSlotId)` at `0x1D5C5`. Its cue is before the native operation.
- Details `btn_AutoEquip.onRelease` starts at `0x1DE14`; it calls `NativePlaySoundFX("MenuConfirm",1)` at `0x1DE2A` before `NativeInvAutoEquipSlot(InvSlotId)` at `0x1DE49`. The ALL-banner uses a separate `NativeInvAutoEquipSlot(-1)` path; this evidence does not establish a sound for that path.
- `NativeInvEquipItem` IDA wrapper at `0x43E600` checks argument count at `0x43E610`, resolves and validates the source item at `0x43E620`–`0x43E688`, then enters deeper mutation code. `NativeInvUnequipItem` at `0x43EF64` checks arity and resolves the equipment slot/player before its mutation call. `NativeInvAutoEquipSlot` at `0x43DA2C` checks arity, resolves the slot and player, and dispatches the best-item path. The wrapper-level checks and downstream requirement/current-owner gates mean UI admission and mutation success are distinct. The SWF's cue statements are unconditional once their respective admitted handlers reach them, so a cue must not be conditioned on the PC mutation returning `true`.
- The ledger maps `PickupArmor` source ordinal 154 to XML UID 152, `PickupPotion` ordinal 156 to UID 153, `PickupWeapon` ordinal 157 to UID 154, and `MenuConfirm` ordinal 141 to UID 2. Existing B122 notes establish the shared named-UI route through `RuntimeSessionAudioV1` → `RuntimeAudioHostV1` → the retained mixer/WinMM output; they do not prove these new action callers or their audibility.

**Expected behavior.** A valid, enabled Details release gets one source cue for the admitted handler: weapon slots 1/2 use `PickupWeapon`; potion category 9 uses `PickupPotion`; the remaining armor/ring categories use `PickupArmor`. Equip requests the cue after the native attempt and authored list/display refresh; Unequip requests it before the native attempt. Details AutoEquip requests `MenuConfirm` before its slot operation. Audio failure must not block the operation. Disabled, stale, invalid, or background releases that do not reach the authored handler get no cue. One admitted release emits one cue; redraws, held input, render receipts, and ALL-banner dispatch must not create additional cues.

## Minimal integration recipe

1. Add a typed, optional action-cue callback to `RuntimeEquipmentPageBindingsV1` / `NativeEquipmentActions`, with action kind and the selected source `InvSlotId`. Route it from `MainPage::release` at the source call boundary. For Equip, call it after the `native_actions_.equip(...)` attempt even if that callback returns false, because the original SWF does not branch on its void native result. For Unequip, call it before `native_actions_.unequip(...)`. Invoke it only after the existing DetailsPresenter admission, owned-instance/currentness, exact ItemTable, requirement, same-owner, and effect gates have admitted the action; do not emit from generic row focus or a render-change receipt.
2. In the existing `main.cpp` pending-command branch, emit `MenuConfirm` immediately before `runtimeEquipment->auto_equip_slot(pending.source_slot, error)` only for `request_auto_equip`. Preserve its one-shot selected-instance/slot command admission and let the action proceed if audio submission fails. Do not add a cue to `request_auto_equip_all` without separate original caller evidence.
3. Resolve the action to names through the existing shared `play_named_ui_sound` API, and log cue name, ordinal, UID, token, status, source slot, and frame. Do not hardcode UID or substitute a sample. The integration must preserve B123/B125 current-instance, ItemTable row, and item-effect validation.

Current `MainPage::release` executes equip/unequip synchronously in `features/equipment/equipment_main_page.cpp` and returns only a command enum. `RuntimeEquipmentPageV1::release_for_source` stores only pending auto-equip/drop/transmute requests and render receipts. Thus the proposed callback is needed to preserve both source order and the no-success-only-cue behavior; submitting later from the generic source-composition return path would lose the command type and could suppress a source cue after a rejected native mutation.
