## Expected behavior and proposed repair

Preserve the authored touch drag/animated list-index behavior and the separate category rail arrows. For the requested PC adaptation, route mouse wheel over the list, Up/Down (or PageUp/PageDown) while the merchant is open, and a vertical pointer drag that starts and ends in the list to adjacent row-window changes through the same ShopUi selection API. Keep the focused row and detail pane synchronized. Clamp movement at first/last row, consistent with the authored `loop=false` default and `getValidIndex` invalid-index behavior; no new rows or ordering should be invented. Ignore wheel/drag/key events while confirmation/error modals own input; ignore wheel outside the visible list and drag unless the pointer began inside the list, and do nothing for empty/single-row categories. Consume recognized merchant navigation keys so they do not leak to gameplay. These PC input gestures are adaptations; they are not claims about original desktop controls.
