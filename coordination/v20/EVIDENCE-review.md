## P17 evidence and blockers

- **Transitions:** TRANSITIONS2-report.md documents the IDA route (TriggerZoneExitLevel::Update 0x39c33c → HUD DisplayFastTravel; confirm calls NativeGoToZone 0x4422b8) and the relevant zone fields. Its intermediate E-key implementation was superseded: P17INT-report.md confirms the final integration uses **Space**, consistent with the user’s single context button, with prompt/candidate priority and test hooks. The real EXE logs confirm Space transitions, Darkwood round trip, and procedural witch-cave load/seed reuse. The prompt art and exact confirm behavior are not visible in the sampled video, and the panel is a placeholder. SafeZone kinds 16/17 are now implemented in final P17: `original_campaign_world_adapter.cpp:62-63` routes them to the provider, `campaign_host.cpp:166-172` clears/sets safe-zone state and calls the music callback, and `main.cpp:2824` binds that callback to runtime audio. This matches IDA `Script_EnterSafeZone::IsBlocking` 0x455668 and `LeaveSafeZone::IsBlocking` 0x455670 (both non-blocking), with Execute at 0x45fc80/0x45fbe0 calling `VoxSoundManager::SetInSafeZoneMusic` 0x36c014. `P17FIX-report.md` and source show this was merged by final head `955d486bf3594572f7f764ecbb0b295d1b590559` (the implementation commit is an ancestor). Treat it as implemented; capture evidence remains source/test-backed, not a new visual review in this audit.
- **Conditions, doors, and restore:** the condition evaluator’s truth table is source-backed by IDA Condition_IsQuestInStateGeneric::Eval 0x478fe8 and integrated logs exercise a Witch-state flip. The “kill” is injected with a property-435 test aid, not a live combat producer. The three unresolved authored names from the original CONDITIONS report remain `IsLowerThanDW_Phase_3`, `IsDuring_Darkwood_Ring`, and `IsBefore_FireTemp_GoToHellgate`; each is absent from the retained v2Conditions name table. Separately, `RENE_FOLLOW` is authored row 115 (so it is not one of those three), with factory op=1, quest row 8, threshold 13. Original `v2quests_pyarraynames.bin` maps row 8 to `Darkwoods_GoToCathedral`; parsing its source v2Quest record yields authored initial state 0, so the authentic predicate is `13 > quest[8].state`, true for a fresh profile. `AuthoredConditionsV1::evaluate` implements op=1 as `argument_c > state` (`features/conditions/authored_conditions_v1.cpp:96-109`); `CharacterQuestProgressV1::initialize_fresh` seeds from the authored definition state (`features/quests/character_quest_progress_v1.cpp:68-81`), and the population caller loads that same CharacterState's QuestRuntime before applying the gate (`main.cpp:1397-1430`). False/unavailable admission is deferred or excluded by the population policy (`main.cpp:1433+`). The later integration assertion that `RENE_FOLLOW` itself is absent was mistaken; remove its `--condition-active` override to exercise the recovered gate. A resumed profile at state 13 or later should correctly hide Rene. Later DOORS2 evidence supersedes the earlier “owner stub” note for door commands/visual state. **Door collision is unimplemented:** `door_runtime_v1.hpp:15-16` says the world floor owner is unbound and `collision_closed()` is only an accessor. **Fresh-process restore is implemented:** `VERIFY17-report.md` documents and tests the startup-order defect fix at final head `955d486...` (ancestor `d87f5b10`); main binds quest runtime without replayed banners on resume and skips campaign-trigger feed during restore. It logs restored open wooden/fire doors and current quest 50 without replaying caption/script/banner, while containers stay broken. Door/lever sound ordinal identity and the fire-door glow origin remain uncertain. There is no waterfall declaration gated by the Witch in the Swamp; the sole checked waterfall is Darkwood’s Wormroot Hollow at Part 2 ~909 s.
- **Procedural generation:** PROCGEN2 reports exact agreement against the original ARM oracle for 1,378 RNG rows and 70 layout outcomes (seeds 0 and 1), plus a real menu-to-witch-cave run. The reference sequence at Part 1 ~850–945 s supports the general cave vocabulary/structure, not exact layout because its seed is unknown. Cave caustics are static rather than animated; cave camera height differs; several map/decor visuals remain unverified. Treat this as strong logic parity with incomplete visual parity.
- **Release integration:** P17 worktree is based on older p16/integrate, while B069–B077 fixes live on p16.1/integrate; neither report verifies their combined frozen v20 executable. The v20 handoff still lists B068 as unfinished and unmerged. A final merged build, relevant normal-path checks, and independent verification remain required before release closure.

## Reviewer disposition

Evidence supports resolving B069, B070, B073 (visual scope), B074 as no divergence, and B077’s following behavior after the final candidate’s integrated checks, subject to resolving the packaged `RENE_FOLLOW` override against actual profile state. Keep B071, B072 and B075 explicitly partial. B076 is a caveated visual fix. SafeZone, fresh-process door restore, and quest-state condition evaluation are implemented in final P17 source; door collision remains open. Do not use component tests or the per-fix EXEs as proof of the combined v20 release. B068 and the remaining P17 integration/visual gaps above remain release blockers or documented caveats according to root’s selected v20 scope.
