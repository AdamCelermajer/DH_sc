## Original first (what the original does)
- Skip: `ScriptManager::SkipScript` 0x4604c0 only sets the global skip id (and flushes queued dialog); `ScriptManager::ExecuteScript` 0x45c16c then runs every remaining command with `skip = (*this != -1)`, does NOT consult `IsBlocking` while skipping (except ExecScript) and so runs the rest of the script to its end in the same frame. Each `Execute(skip)` decides itself: `Script_PlayActorAnim::Execute` (0x45e890) and `Script_PlayAnimByName` do nothing when skipping; `Script_StartDialog` enqueues nothing; `Script_PlayCamera::Execute` (0x460110) plays the level set's idle camera clip instead; `Script_SetCameraTarget` uses transition 0; `Script_PutCharacterInIdle::Execute` (0x45d590) sets `waitForAnim = !skip`; `Script_SetActorPosition::Execute` (0x45e5c8) and `Script_LookActor::Execute` (0x45ec50) ignore the flag (state is applied, `SM_SetIdleState` precedes the position). So the original does NOT apply the final pose of the skipped actor clips: the skipped actors simply stay in Idle at the placed position/heading.
- Target bar: `InfoHUDManager::FastUpdate` 0x41e064 shows `HUDelements.HealthBars.enemy` while `CharAI::AI_GetTargetAsCharacter` of the local player (or the Character+5284 object of interest) is a monster and hides it only when that target is gone. Nothing there depends on where the monster is on screen. `CharAI::_UpdateTarget` 0x3cb908 clears the AI target (event 12) when the target's vtable+136 validity check fails (not targetable, e.g. the Mud Lord dive after `SetIsTargetable(false)`): the bar legitimately disappears during the dive.
- Death outro: `Swamp_Serpent_Defeated` (decoded from `original-campaign.xml`, 104 commands): every actor (hero, priest, faery, boss) is placed at `_prim_Waypoint_Center`, the camera target is that waypoint, then `PlayActorAnim` clips 549/550/551 (`cs_swampking_outro_swampking_scene01/02/03`, 4699 ms, 2333 ms, 33 ms hold pose) pose the dead boss, camera clips 504..510 (authored relative to the Center rig) frame the dialog. The last command moves the boss to `_prim_Waypoint_kinghider` and hides it. `SM_SetAnimState` has no dead-guard (like `SM_SetIdleState` 0x3c1a00, already noted in B155).
- Speaker plate: the DialogActors row of actor 36 resolves to `$player`, replaced by the character name. The original footage `C:/dh217/video/sheets/s1_14.jpg` shows the same plate reading the player's name (CRISR82 in that recording).

## (b) Target bar vanishes: WORKED
Cause (reproduced): `main.cpp` anchors the bar bottom-centre on the projected head of the target (head = actor position + static bounds height x scale, about +780 for the Mud Lord) and `project()` returned false when that point was outside the viewport, so the bar was skipped. The ring is drawn from the same target, so a rearing/rising boss near the top edge showed ring and no bar. With the head hovering at the edge the bar flickered every frame.
- Before (old exe, `--target-hud-trace`): `C:/dh217/cases/w5m2-skip/run.log` line 14783 `[target-hud] frame=1223 target=<boss> ... headProjected=0` and no change until the target is lost: the bar is never drawn while the boss is targeted. Frames `C:/dh217/cases/w5m2-skip/f2100.png`, `f2470.png`, `f2600.png` (boss at the top edge, ring under it, "Miss" popping, NO bar) are the owner's screenshot 1.
- Flicker (fixed exe logging `headOnScreen`): `C:/dh217/cases/w5m2k1/run.log`: `frame=544 headOnScreen=0`, `545 =1`, `546 =0` (screen y -1.8 / +2.6 / -0.8), `553 =1`, `563 =0`, `571 =1`, `572 =0` ... With the old code the bar was absent in every `=0` frame.
- Fix: `features/hud_panels/target_bar_anchor_v1.hpp` (`target_bar_anchor_v1`, `target_bar_origin_v1`) and `main.cpp` (`project_clip` extracted from `project`; the bar now uses the anchor): the projected position is kept even outside the viewport and the bar is clamped fully into the window; a head behind the eye anchors at the top centre. The bar is therefore drawn whenever the boss is the retained target.
- After: `C:/dh217/cases/w5m2f-skip/f1374.png` (boss mostly above the screen, ring visible, bar "Mud Lord" with level 2 clamped at the top), log `barShown=1 headOnScreen=0 screen=...,-259`.
- Test: `target_bar_anchor_v1` (on-screen centre, head above the viewport clamps to the top edge, corner, behind-eye, non-finite and tiny window).
- Not changed (original behaviour): while the boss is untargetable during its dive the AI target is cleared (see above), so the bar is hidden for about 150 frames per dive (`target=none` lines in the logs); it returns when `TargetAgain` runs.
- Other suspects checked and rejected: ES-BUFF selection-only targetability (`eligible=1 suppressed=0` throughout the fight in the trace), `eligible_target` conflating acquire/attack (damage and ring unaffected), HUD owner relocation (the bar is drawn from the same marker target as the ring).

## (c) Death cutscene camera: WORKED
Cause (reproduced with `--kill-test _prim_Boss_SwampKing_01@200` plus `--quest-debug-kill-property 201:358:1`, which starts `Swamp_Serpent_Defeated` deterministically at frame 203): the camera tracks (rig = Center, clip eye e.g. (+386,+315,+190) looking back through Center) are authored for the boss LYING at Center. In the port the dead boss never took the three outro clips: `CombatSession::play_actor_clip` interrupted the runtime's Died pose, but `ActorCombatRuntime::synchronize` re-asserts Died for every dead actor that does not own a pose and `selectedClip` stayed `.../Died/sequence-0/phase-0`; the Died clip restarted exactly at each PlayActorAnim (frames 203, 463, 603; pose bounds hi.z 776..860 = standing, period 260 frames) and the update loop skipped dead actors. The body stood upright at Center and filled the camera. The pose probe (`--clip-probe`) shows the authored clips lay the body down: hi.z 168 at the end of scene01 and for scene02/03 (z -336..168).
- Before: `C:/dh217/cases/w5m2k6/f630.png` (identical to the owner's screenshot 4: green column, nameplate, SKIP) and `f555.png`; log `C:/dh217/cases/w5m2k9/run.log` `pose{runtimeOwns=1 ... selectedClip=.../Died/...}` at frames 210..650.
- Fix (`combat_session.cpp`): `play_actor_clip` on a dead actor hands the pose over with `runtime->yield_pose` (the same hand-over the Despawn owner uses), and the update loop advances a script-driven clip (stateManaged, no state sequence, lifecycle state 14) on a dead actor. General for every scripted clip on a dead Character.
- After: `C:/dh217/cases/w5m2k10/f630.png` (priest and hero side by side, boss body lying low at the right edge, same composition as the original footage `C:/dh217/video/sheets/s1_14.jpg` / `s1_16.jpg`), `f555.png` (boss lying at the lower left); log `C:/dh217/cases/w5m2k10/run.log`: `pose{runtimeOwns=0 stateManaged=1 selectedClip=cs:549/cs:550/cs:551}` and `poseBounds ... hi.z 167.983` at frames 470..650.
- Test: `run_scripted_clip_on_dead_actor` (kills the actor, plays a clip twice, steps 45 frames each, checks the runtime never re-owns the pose and the actor stays dead). Fails without the fix.
- Not verified frame by frame against the original footage (only the composition at "Alright, I've followed you this far" and "What?"); the camera clips themselves are unchanged.
