## 1. Why no Rogue damage was seen (blockers 1 and 2 of the first report)

Investigated the original first. In the Android pseudocode export (`libDungeonHunter2.so/pseudocode-all.c`, `CharAI::AI_DoMeleeAttack`, line 149677, 0x3d01ac) the tail is: with a target set, `if(!CharAI::AI_IsInMeleeRange) return;` and only then `CharStateMachine::SM_SetAttackState`. The port's `character_ai_attack.cpp` line 74 (`if(s.target&&!c.query(attack_current_in_melee))return 0;`) is the same rule, and the game's own tutorial caption says "Red circles will appear underneath enemies within attack range" (frame `l01/f1700.png` shows the tutorial; the red ring under the Bogwomp in `l01/f2200.png`). So Tab on a target out of melee range followed by Space does nothing. This is original behaviour, not a bug and not Rogue specific. (IDA through the rea MCP was not usable: `open_binary` rejects the `.i64`; the exported pseudocode in `.local-inputs/ida-apk-export-2026-10-07` was used.)

Why r06 of the first session had 0 combo boundaries and 0 damage, all three causes were harness faults, shown in its log:
1. `--player-to -2900,-100,156` put the hero 0.7 below the `_prim_TriggerZone_LizManIntro` floor (zone z 156.739..356.739). The log has `Actor final ... RoguePlayerBase ... position=-2900,-100,156 grounded=0` and no `trigger _prim_TriggerZone_LizManIntro` line, so the lizard intro never started (the first report mistook the Swamp_Intro cinematic for it). z=255 works (`Player-to position=-2900,-100,255` then `trigger _prim_TriggerZone_LizManIntro -> script LizardMan_Intro started`).
2. Tab picked `_prim_LizTemplate_01` at (-4913,-1441), 2,400 units away. Out of melee range, so the original rule above gives no attack (`attackActive=0` in every STATE_TRACE input edge).
3. After the intro the CombatTuto tutorial (6 captions) eats the first Space presses.

The "outgoing attacks await their own source pipeline" message (`main.cpp` ~1154) is stale: the profile `animationOnly=true` there is only a menu-time placeholder; the "Source player melee bank" block (~1810) replaces it with `animationOnly=false`, `sourceCombo`, dual-dagger markers. The message was reworded (code change, section 6). The combat-session guards "Animation-only actor rejects combat commands" do not apply to the Rogue player.

## 4. Ranked blocker list

1. **The Rogue cannot kill the Mud Lord on its own HP.** 177 max HP against 87-damage bites and a 1300 HP boss, no potions left after the Witch, melee only. Not shown whether this is the original's balance (the Knight has 169 HP, also needed the follow aid, and won in mudlord3 runs m3f2, m3f4, m3f5 but died in m3f3) or a Rogue defense/stat gap (damage reduction from armor was not compared). Needs a stat comparison of the Rogue against the original (Dodge/armor/HP).
2. **Walking is untested.** All area changes are teleports. The Mud Lord and the Witch relocate; the follow aid hides the original's approach problem (B158 drift cause still unknown).
3. **The shipped v21.8 staged package lacks files the Rogue/Act 1 path needs.** (a) 5 weapon swoosh FX (`swoosh_prince_dual_combo_01..03`, `swoosh_prince_2hand_combo_01..02`): log `Required source animation step FX ... swoosh_prince_dual_combo_01.bdae not found` 28 times in l01, 0 with the files. (b) 10 Mud Lord files (`scripts/ai/swampking_core.luac`, 5 `swamp_king_*` skill scripts, 2 `fx_swampking_*`, `spell_dh2_hurt_steam`, `elemental_bolt_water`): without them `[boss-ai] profile=SwampKing swampking_core owner unavailable` and the boss never attacks (first-session r05 log). Both lists are now groups `rogue_dual_swoosh_fx` and `mud_lord_ai_and_fx` in `coordination/v21.8/asset-selection-v21.8.json`; the existing staged folder was not touched (the stage script never modifies an existing folder), the runs use a copy. A new staged package must be generated for a shipped build.
4. **Seed stats confound blocker 1.** The hand-built Rogue seeds are "Rogue Lvl: 9", Strength 5, Dexterity 10, Endurance 6, Energy 5, HP 146/177, MP 40/50, armor 4, defense rating 77, damage 6-12 and 6-11 (`h01/f950.png`, Stats page, viewed), 0 unspent points. A level-9 Rogue that has never spent attribute points is much weaker than a player at that level, so the Mud Lord result says little about the original balance. Not compared with the Knight afterwitch seed.
   Training the other three skills through the Skills page was not run; Vicious Strike was (section 2, row 3).
5. **Stun/push have no host state** (carried from skills-a, JumpKick `outcomes=0x80` push and Vicious stun `0x40` are rolled and logged but do nothing).
6. **Log spam in Rogue runs:** `Mage skill projectile executor unavailable: ... original-cache/data/pydata/dialogs_pyarray.bin` 2,047 lines in l01. The v21.8 staged package has `dialogs_pyarray.bin` only under `assets/data/pydata`, not under `original-cache/data/pydata` (the Mage package has it). Harmless for the Rogue.
7. **Harness hazards:** `--player-to` with coordinates from another level (cave coordinates used in the Swamp) ends the run with `Foundation error: Native body differs from shared actor position` (w01, w04). Space is also the transition confirm, so Space taps next to an exit zone take the exit. Sync-file anchors are needed between phases because wall-clock time and frame time differ (about 67 frames/s here).
