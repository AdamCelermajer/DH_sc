# B038 - HUD XP bar never fills after kills

Status: implemented and isolated-tested. Not closed: integrated runtime/visual verification (root build and verifier) is still required. Level-up visual is not yet observed in the reference video.

## 1. Evidence

### Visual (reference video, v1.0.3 Act 1 walkthrough)
Frames extracted under `.local-inputs/claude-preview12/B038/` (git-ignored). Observed, enlarged top-left HUD crops:
- The XP bar is the thin green strip directly above the red HP bar and blue MP bar, top-left, beside the portrait.
- Observed: the green fill starts at the left end of the bar and the unfilled remainder is dark on the right. Growth is left-to-right.
- t=355-363 s: fill ends at roughly 84% of the bar length (approximate, read from enlarged crops; not measured by pixel script).
- t=364-365 s: fill steps right to roughly 90%, following the tutorial kill sequence (the "+5 EXP" floating text appears at t=377 s in `full_377.png`).
- t=374-375 s: fill at roughly 95%.
- From about t=376 s the tutorial "SKIP" overlay and inventory screens cover the HUD, so no level-up was seen in this window.
- Inference: the visible fill tracks the XP sheet ratio. A level-up reset was NOT observed and is not verified. A pixel-script measurement was attempted but was too noisy with overlays, so the numbers above are visual estimates.

### Logic (IDA and port code)
- `InfoHUDManager::FastUpdate` at `.local-inputs/ida-apk-export-2026-10-07/libraries/libDungeonHunter2.so/pseudocode-all.c:206031`. Verified lines around 206031+150..168:
  - `xp = 100 * *(int*)(this_char+4220) / *(int*)(this_char+4224)`, then `if (xp >= 99) xp = 99`, then `GotoFrame(bar_xp, xp)` at `this+204`.
  - HP and MP use `(v7-1) & ~(v7-1 >> 31)` (i.e. `100*cur/max - 1`, clamped at 0), then cap 99. XP has no `-1` and no lower clamp in the code. The C division is signed.
  - Offsets 4220/4224 are sheet indices 33 and 34, since HP uses 4232/4240 = indices 36/38 under the same `4088 + 4*index` mapping.
- Port level-up carry: `port/level-world/player_progression_v1.cpp:87-90` and `port/windows-foundation/features/loot/runtime_death_rewards_v1.cpp:60,367`. Kill rewards write through `world.update_combat_properties(player.id, ...)`, which is the same store read by `combat_properties(...)`.
- The sheet is `std::array<std::int32_t,224>` and holds x256 fixed-point values (`asr8`). The ratio `100*xp/need` cancels the scale. The 32-bit wrap of `100*xp` mirrors the ARM code.
- Source timeline: `reports/hud-source.json` `sprite_timelines` entry id 152 has 101 frames, 102 placements. Frame 100 is never selected by the original producer, so frames 0..99 only.
- Authored geometry (480x320, probed): bar_bg 148 at x[50,105] y[21,25]; cover 150 at the same bounds. Frame 0 covers all; frame 50 covers the right half; frame 99 covers only the far right. HP/MP covers are 88/145 in the same style.

### Expected behaviour
- The XP bar fills left to right in proportion to `XP / XP-for-level`, capped at 99 (empty at 0).
- A level-up carries `XP - previous threshold` into the new level, so the bar restarts at the carry fraction of the new threshold.
- Non-XP HUD geometry is unchanged by the XP frame.

## 5. Uncertainties and what the verifier and root must check
- Level-up reset visually: not observed. Verifier should find a level-up in the reference video and compare the bar (carry fraction) right after it.
- Integrated screen check: launch an isolated window with a fresh save (not the user's live game). Kill enemies and watch the top-left XP strip above the HP bar. It should grow left to right and reset after a level-up.
- Real XP sheet: the rendered frame depends on the live `resolved[33]/[34]` values. Verifier should confirm the kill rewards change the frame during play (the `update_combat_properties` path was read, not run).
- No-player fallback (XP 0, empty bar) is my choice, not verified original behaviour. HP/MP still use the existing fallback.
- Original bar screen position and scale are derived from the authored 480x320 HUD and the same scale used for HP/MP. The exact on-screen alignment against the video was not checked pixel-for-pixel.
- Fixed-point: `resolved` values are x256. The formula is scale-invariant, but the 32-bit wrap is mirrored literally; this matters only for very large XP.
