# B138 original-behaviour study: dropped-item label, pickup, chest drop, gold vs item paths

Investigator: B138 session (read-only on source). Consumer: B137 owner (owns main.cpp). Date 2026-10-12, repo HEAD 45a0c602, branch windows-foundation.
Status: investigation only. No tracked source edited. Scratch: `.local-inputs/v21.4/b138/` (frames `f/s01-s23.png` 5 fps from 209.4 s, `g/c01-c30.png` 10 fps crops from 210.6 s, sheets `sheetA0-3.png`, `crops-210.6.png`).

## 0. Short answers

1. The original has NO floating item name over a ground item and NO icon. The only item-name text is a transient HUD status line (`itemname_text`, rarity colour, fade) that appears when the item is picked up. In our build, the white "Imbued Belt" over the open chest is the P14 invention (`main.cpp:8553-8566`); it is not original behaviour (the code it was modelled on, `ItemObject::ShowTooltip`, is unreachable: `GetInteractionType` always returns -1).
2. The original throws chest loot out of the chest toward the opener (scatter 150-349 units, items stop 80 short of the landing point). Ours leaves chest drops exactly at the chest origin with no travel (`runtime_world_item_adapter_v1.cpp:103,131`; `container_loot_v1.cpp:116`). That is why the belt sits inside the opened chest and the label hangs over the chest.
3. Pickup in the original: walking onto the item (contact begin while the hero is moving) -> `ItemObject::Interact`. No key. There is no radius auto-pickup and no delay after landing (only a 5 s owner window for items the player dropped).
4. Gold vs item: same `Interact`, but in the original and in ours the equippable-only gates (AutoTransmute, `IsInventoryFull`) are skipped for gold. In OUR code the gold branch also skips `Presenter::pickup`, which calls `validate_character_state` on the whole profile; that is the single most likely reason "gold works, items don't" after the transition (hypothesis, not proven; see section 5).

## 3. Expected behaviour (what the player should see)

(a) Name text: none above ground items. On pickup, one status line in the item's rarity colour (white for unpowered, green for 1 power, etc.), centred-left at about 59% screen height, fading in and out over about 0.7 s, queued (max 4) when several items are collected. Gold uses the same line ("4 Gold" is already observed in B128). Inventory-full shows the localized `GAMEPLAYMENUS_INVENTORY_FULL` line (plain, no item colour) and the item stays. No icon anywhere.
(b) Pickup: walkover only. Contact begins while the hero is moving -> Interact once; refusal keeps the item; re-contact retries. Chest/enemy items fly outward toward the player, land 70-270 units away; there is no post-landing delay. Owner window of 5 s only for items the player dropped. Quest/key items (slotting -1) skip the full-bag test; potions respect potion capacity; equippables respect `IsInventoryFull` (100) and AutoTransmute. Pickup counter, `loot_orb_fx`, pickup sound, despawn on success; nothing remains on the ground or in the chest.
(c) Chest: after opening it stays open and empty with no marker or label. The loot table is `Loot` field of the authored OpenableContainer row (Swamp_Normal_Chest: table 227). All items are thrown out, none stays inside.
(d) Gold and item share Interact; gold is simply exempt from the equippable-only checks.

## 5. Why gold can work while items do not (separate paths, in ours)

Gold in the original: same Interact, skipping slotting-based checks. In ours, differences between the two branches are exactly: (1) `character->inventory.size() >= 100` (equippable only), (2) potion capacity (potions only), (3) `Presenter::pickup` with whole-profile validation and ItemTable lookup (items only), (4) unique-instance-id derivation (`world-drop-N`, store counter restarts each session, handled by a suffix loop). Gold touches none of them.

Reported symptoms after the Bogwitch transition: items not picked up, potion click plays sound but potion not used, Equipment tab dead. All three consumers call `validate_character_state` on the live profile: `Presenter::pickup` (`inventory_feature.cpp:11,47`), `Presenter::present` for the Equipment/inventory page (`inventory_feature.cpp:28`), and `runtime_session_potion_use_v1.cpp:79`. Gold bypasses it. Hypothesis (NOT verified): after the transition `validate_character_state(state)` returns errors (candidate fields it checks: schema/ids, `health > max_health`, `resource > max_resource`, equipment bindings referencing instances absent from inventory, duplicate equipment slots, `menu_metadata`, `visited_modules` sort order, `components` (entry component set at `main.cpp:9190`), and `validate_character_quest_blob(state.id, source_quest_progress_cqpg)`). Cheapest discriminating test: at the failed pickup log `validate_character_state(*sharedCharacter).errors` and `pickup.error`/`receipt` text, and confirm `sharedCharacter` is the same object as `state` (the log prints `state.inventory.size()` as "stacks" while the transfer writes through `sharedCharacter`; unchanged stacks with outcome=failed would point here). Ruled out for the item-only symptom by code reading: AutoTransmute (option never bound), `rules.potion_capacity` (not a belt gate), owner window (chest drops have owner invalid).
